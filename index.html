    One round from take-off to vanish

    Each round follows a fixed pattern. A betting window opens, players place their stakes, and the plane begins to climb as the multiplier rises. At a point set in advance by the game’s random system, the plane disappears and the round ends. Anyone who cashed out earlier keeps stake times the multiplier shown at that moment; anyone still in the round loses the stake.

    That departure point is locked before take-off, so nothing done mid-round can alter it. Past flights do not create a pattern you can use, which is why thorfortune casino review pages usually focus on timing discipline rather than prediction. In practice, the only real choice is when to leave.

    Keeping the balance airborne

    • Set a session budget before the first round and stop when it is gone, no matter what the history ribbon shows.
    • Keep single stakes near one or two percent of the bankroll so a cold run does not end the session early.
    • Pick the exit multiplier before take-off; deciding while the curve is rising is where discipline usually slips.
    • Avoid chasing losses by increasing stakes, because the game has no memory of the previous round.
    • Take timed breaks. Rounds are short, and repeated play can make small mistakes add up quickly.
